What is Overreliance?

LLMs produce fluent, confident text — even when wrong. Overreliance is trusting that output without verification.

Watch how confidently the bot fabricates facts and invents citations.

Mitigation
  • Ground answers with real retrieval + citations
  • Surface uncertainty / confidence
  • Cross-check critical claims
  • UX that discourages blind trust
